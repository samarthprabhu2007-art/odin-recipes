Panner Masala

Back to Main Page

Description

Paneer Masala is a popular North Indian vegetarian dish featuring succulent cubes of paneer (Indian cottage cheese) simmered in a deeply aromatic, highly seasoned onion-tomato gravy. It is celebrated for its balanced heat and complex profile of traditional spices.

Ingredients

  • Paneer (Indian cottage cheese, cubed)
  • Oil or ghee
  • Onions (finely chopped)
  • Tomatoes (pureed or finely chopped)
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Spices (turmeric, red chili powder, coriander powder, garam masala)
  • Kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
  • Salt
  • Water
  • Fresh coriander (cilantro, optional for garnish)

Steps

  1. Sear paneer cubes in a pan with a little oil until lightly golden, then set aside
  2. Sauté onions in oil or ghee over medium heat until they turn golden brown
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and fry for 1 minute until the raw smell disappears
  4. Pour tomato puree into the pan and cook until the oil starts separating from the masala
  5. Stir in spices (turmeric, chili, coriander powder, and salt) and cook for another minute
  6. Add water to adjust the gravy thickness and bring it to a gentle simmer
  7. Slide in paneer cubes and simmer covered for 3–4 minutes so they absorb the flavors
  8. Finish the dish by stirring in garam masala and crushed kasuri methi
  9. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot
