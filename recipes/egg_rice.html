Egg Rice

Back to Main Page

Description

Egg fried rice is a classic, globally beloved staple of Chinese cuisine (specifically originating from Southern China, most famously Yangshou/Yangzhou). At its core, it is a high-heat stir-fry dish that transforms simple, humble components—typically leftover rice, eggs, aromatics, and soy sauce—into a highly fragrant, savory, and satisfying meal.

Ingredients

  • Day-old cooked rice (chilled)
  • Eggs
  • Vegetable or peanut oil
  • Garlic
  • Green onions (scallions)
  • Light soy sauce
  • Toasted sesame oil
  • White pepper
  • Salt
  • Carrots & peas (optional)

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a wok or large pan over high heat
  2. Sauté garlic and the white parts of the green onions for 30 seconds
  3. Add carrots and peas, stir-frying for 2 minutes until softened
  4. Push veggies aside, pour whisked eggs into the empty space, and scramble gently
  5. Add cold rice, breaking up any clumps with your spatula
  6. Toss and fry everything together on maximum heat for 3 minutes
  7. Drizzle soy sauce around the edges of the pan and mix well
  8. Season with white pepper, salt, the green onion tops, and a splash of sesame oil
  9. Toss once more and serve hot
